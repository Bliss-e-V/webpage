In June, we started *Ask Me Anything* (AMA), our first regular event on the BLISS Discord server. A guest joins the voice channel and answers your questions about their research, their career path or anything else. You can post questions in advance in the #ask-me-anything channel or ask them live.

Some of our guests so far:

- **Tom Neuhäuser** (9 June), BLISS president – Doing a PhD in Berlin and machine learning for cognitive science and medicine.
- **Lorenz Hufe** (7 July), BLISS founding member and ELLIS PhD candidate at Fraunhofer HHI and the University of Oxford – AI for history, and doing a PhD at Oxford.
- **Linus Folkerts** (29 July), former BLISS member, now testing the cyber capabilities of AI models at the AI Security Institute – How to get into AI for cybersecurity.

Want to be there next time? [Join our Discord server](https://discord.gg/QrbZtHb3sK). We announce each AMA there and on LinkedIn.
