[Jane Street](https://www.janestreet.com/) is a research-driven trading firm with offices in New York, Chicago, London, Amsterdam, Hong Kong and Singapore. Its traders, researchers and engineers use mathematics, statistics, machine learning and a lot of code to work on hard problems in financial markets.

BLISS is a non-profit association, and all our events are free to attend. Support from partners like Jane Street makes this possible: our [Speaker Series](/speaker-series) with researchers from all over the world, hands-on [workshops](/workshops) and hackathons, and the pizza and drinks after the talks, where many of the best conversations happen.

Curious about Jane Street? Try one of their [monthly puzzles](https://www.janestreet.com/puzzles/), or have a look at their [internships and open positions](https://www.janestreet.com/join-jane-street/).

Thank you, Jane Street, for supporting the AI community in Berlin! Would your organization like to support BLISS too? Write to us at [partners@bliss.berlin](mailto:partners@bliss.berlin).
