[Jane Street](https://www.janestreet.com/) is a global trading firm with offices in New York, London, Hong Kong, Singapore and Amsterdam. Its traders, researchers and engineers use mathematics, statistics, machine learning and a lot of code to work on hard problems in financial markets.

## What the sponsorship makes possible

BLISS is a non-profit association, and all our events are free to attend. Support from partners like Jane Street makes this possible for:

- our [Speaker Series](/speaker-series), with researchers from all over the world
- hands-on [workshops](/workshops) and hackathons
- pizza and drinks after the talks, where many of the best conversations happen

## Curious about Jane Street?

Try one of their [monthly puzzles](https://www.janestreet.com/puzzles/), or have a look at their [internships and open positions](https://www.janestreet.com/join-jane-street/).

Thank you, Jane Street, for supporting the AI community in Berlin! Would your organization like to support BLISS too? Write to us at [partners@bliss.berlin](mailto:partners@bliss.berlin).
