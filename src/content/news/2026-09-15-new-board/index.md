At our general assembly, the members of BLISS e.V. elected a new board:

- **Tom Neuhäuser** – Chair
- **Marvin Beese** – Vice Chair
- **Louis Pfeiffer** – Finance
- **Frederic Ndjiki-Nya** – Education and Research

A big thank you to everyone who served on the previous board! You can meet the whole team on our [team page](/team).
