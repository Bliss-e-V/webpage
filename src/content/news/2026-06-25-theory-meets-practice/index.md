1. Opening the Black Box: A Hands-On Guide to Explainable AI for LLMs
2. Giving Eyes to your AI: Engineering Multimodal Agents with Haystack
3. Embedded AI – Deep Learning Models on 5€ microcontrollers
4. Explainable Scientific Discovery with AI Memory
5. AI for Precision Medicine: From Foundation Models to Tumor Tissue Profiling
