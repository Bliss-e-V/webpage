Our trailer gives a first hint: this winter, eight speakers from five cities in four countries come to Berlin for the BLISS Speaker Series. Any guesses who's coming?

- **When:** Tuesdays at 7 pm, starting 3 November 2026
- **Where:** TU Berlin, room C130, except 17 November at the Mathematics Building (Str. des 17. Juni 136). See the [updated lineup](/news/speaker-series-winter-2026-27-lineup) for details.
- **Entry:** free

**Update:** The full lineup is out! Meet all eight speakers in our [lineup announcement](/news/speaker-series-winter-2026-27-lineup). Follow the [BLISS Speaker Series on Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/bliss-speaker-series/) to get the invitations. Until the first talk, you can rewatch the 15 talks of the last two semesters on our [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@bliss.ev.berlin).
