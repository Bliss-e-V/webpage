Next to the Speaker Series, we ran hands-on events with our partners this winter:

- **Hackathon with Lyceum and Merantix Momentum** (23 October) – Take a baseline neural network and make it faster and better in two hours, with GPU acceleration software from Lyceum. €1000 for the best model, at the Merantix AI Campus.
- **InstaDeep: Foundational Models for Genomics** (11 December) – Miguel Arbesú, Bernardo Almeida and Jonas Hirsch showed how foundation models decode genomic sequences. Data scientists, biologists and AI enthusiasts filled the room.
- **dida: Theory Meets Practice #1 and #2** (7 and 21 January) – Explainable AI for LLMs with Reduan Achtibat (Fraunhofer HHI), and multimodal agents with Haystack with Bilge Yücel (deepset). More in our post on the [Theory Meets Practice series](/news/theory-meets-practice).
- **Google: Unleash the Power of Agentic AI** (3 February) – A hands-on workshop with Diana Nanova from Google.

Thank you to Lyceum, Merantix Momentum, InstaDeep, dida and Google, and to everyone who brought a laptop! See all workshops on the [workshops page](/workshops).
