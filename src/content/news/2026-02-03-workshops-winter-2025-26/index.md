Talks bring new ideas, but sometimes you want to write the code yourself. That is what our workshops are for: a partner from industry or research brings a hands-on session, and you bring your laptop. This winter, we had five of them:

- **Hackathon with Lyceum and Merantix Momentum** (23 October, Merantix AI Campus) – Two hours to make a baseline neural network faster and better with GPU acceleration software from Lyceum. There was €1,000 for the best model.
- **InstaDeep: Foundational Models for Genomics** (11 December) – Miguel Arbesú, Bernardo Almeida and Jonas Hirsch showed how foundation models decode genomic sequences and how to use them in practice. The room was a mix of data scientists, biologists and AI enthusiasts.
- **dida: Theory Meets Practice #1** (7 January) – Reduan Achtibat (Fraunhofer HHI) on explainable AI for LLMs with layer-wise relevance propagation. [Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H-kTBiuxtaI).
- **dida: Theory Meets Practice #2** (21 January) – Bilge Yücel (deepset) on multimodal agents with Haystack.
- **Google: Unleash the Power of Agentic AI** (3 February) – A hands-on session with Diana Nanova from Google.

Thank you to Lyceum, Merantix Momentum, InstaDeep, dida and Google, and to everyone who brought a laptop! Theory Meets Practice continued in the summer; read more in our [recap of the series](/news/theory-meets-practice). All workshops are listed on the [workshops page](/workshops).
