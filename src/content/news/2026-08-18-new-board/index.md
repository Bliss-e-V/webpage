At our general assembly on 18 August 2026, the members of BLISS e.V. elected the new board, each position unanimously:

- **Tom Neuhäuser** – Chair
- **Marvin Beese** – Vice Chair
- **Louis Pfeiffer** – Finance
- **Frederic Ndjiki-Nya** – Education and Research

In the past year, more than 30 organizers ran over 50 AI events in Berlin. A big thank you to Lorenz Hufe, who handed over the finance role, and to everyone who makes BLISS happen! You can meet the whole team on our [team page](/team).
