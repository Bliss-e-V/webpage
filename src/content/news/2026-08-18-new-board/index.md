At our general assembly on 18 August 2026, the members of BLISS e.V. elected a new board. Each of the four positions was decided unanimously:

- **Tom Neuhäuser** – Chair (re-elected)
- **Marvin Beese** – Vice Chair (re-elected)
- **Louis Pfeiffer** – Finance (new)
- **Frederic Ndjiki-Nya** – Education and Research (new)

In its report, the board looked back on a busy year: more than 30 organizers ran over 50 AI events in Berlin. A big thank you to Lorenz Hufe and Leo Pinetzki, who leave the board, for all their work!
