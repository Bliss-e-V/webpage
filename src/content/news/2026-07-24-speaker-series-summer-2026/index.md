From April to July, we hosted a talk every two weeks at TU Berlin. The topics went from LLM training at scale and multilingual models to human agency, synthetic biology, vision models and AI safety.

On 14 July, Sophia Sanborn (Stanford University) gave the last talk of the semester. She showed how neural digital twins, deep learning models that predict the responses of real neurons, let researchers run neuroscience experiments in silico.

![Sophia Sanborn (Stanford University) during the last talk of the semester](./cover.jpg)
