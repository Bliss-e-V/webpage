We are happy to announce that [Jane Street](https://www.janestreet.com/) now supports BLISS as a sponsor.

Since 2022, BLISS has brought leading AI researchers to Berlin and built a community of students and young professionals who care about machine learning research. Sponsors like Jane Street make it possible for us to keep all our events free and open to everyone.

**What this means for our community**

- More speaker series talks with international guests
- Joint events and workshops with the Jane Street team
- Snacks and drinks at our events

We look forward to the collaboration. Interested in partnering with BLISS too? Write us at [partners@bliss.berlin](mailto:partners@bliss.berlin).
