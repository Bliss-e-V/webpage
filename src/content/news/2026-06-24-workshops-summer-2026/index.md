This summer semester was our busiest yet for workshops:

- **Semrush: Operating Agentic AI in Production** (16 April) – Saeid Nobakht on testing, observability and operations for reliable agentic systems.
- **JetBrains: Building AI Agents** (30 April) – Our workshop for FLINTA\* folks. Ivan Dolgov and Aral D. from the JetBrains Cloud Platform team showed how to run local LLMs (Gemma via Ollama), orchestrate agents with smolagents and connect them to tools with MCP. The university internet was "challenging", but Ivan and Aral didn't miss a beat.
- **G-Research: Pub Quiz** (8 May) – Maths-based brain teasers and a look into quantitative finance.
- **juna.ai: The Heuristic Scientist** (3 June) – Dr. Nima Siboni on LLMs for open-ended algorithm discovery. Participants built their own small discovery loop.
- **QuantCo: Build Your Own AI Agent** (10 June) – Moritz Wilksch and Pavel Zwerschke on agents that write, compile and fix documents on their own.
- **dida: Theory Meets Practice #3 to #5** (29 April, 27 May and 24 June) – Deep learning on 5€ microcontrollers, explainable scientific discovery with AI memory, and AI for precision medicine. More in our post on the [Theory Meets Practice series](/news/theory-meets-practice).

Thank you to Semrush, JetBrains, G-Research, juna.ai, QuantCo and dida! See all workshops on the [workshops page](/workshops).
