In January, we started *Theory Meets Practice*, a workshop series with the Berlin machine learning company [dida](https://dida.do/). In each session, experts explain a current method, and you code along on your own laptop.

1. **Opening the Black Box: Explainable AI for LLMs** (7 January) – Reduan Achtibat (Fraunhofer HHI) on layer-wise relevance propagation for transformers. [Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H-kTBiuxtaI).
2. **Giving Eyes to your AI: Multimodal Agents with Haystack** (21 January) – Bilge Yücel (deepset, the makers of Haystack) on agents that understand images and text.
3. **Embedded AI: Deep Learning Models on €5 Microcontrollers** (29 April) – Elias Trommer (Infineon) on quantization, inference engines and deploying models to embedded hardware.
4. **Explainable Scientific Discovery with AI Memory** (27 May) – Lazar Obradović (cognee) on turning scientific papers into knowledge graphs to evaluate new hypotheses. [Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3XmrrA1eAtg).
5. **AI for Precision Medicine** (24 June) – Lukas Ruff, Christina Embacher, Jonas Dippel and Maaike Galama (Aignostics) on pathology foundation models and tumor tissue profiling. [Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vvbf_VSNSco).

Thank you to dida for co-hosting the series and to all speakers! All workshops are listed on the [workshops page](/workshops).
