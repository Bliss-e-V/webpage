The lineup is out! This winter, eight researchers from leading AI labs and universities come to Berlin for the BLISS Speaker Series. The topics range from reasoning in LLMs and AI safety to cognition in brains and AI, sovereign AI and the agentic web.

- **When:** Tuesdays at 7 pm, starting 3 November 2026
- **Where:** TU Berlin, room C130
- **Entry:** free, registration on Meetup or Luma required

As always, the discussion continues afterwards over free drinks and pizza.

- **3 November: Badr AlKhamissi** (Google DeepMind): Human-Like Artificial Intelligence, register on [Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/bliss-speaker-series/events/316827513) or [Luma](https://luma.com/j2k7yyd4)
- **10 November: Pavel Izmailov** (Anthropic): Understanding Reasoning from Pretraining to Post-Training, register on [Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/bliss-speaker-series/events/316827553) or [Luma](https://luma.com/xg3pn866)
- **17 November: Jonas Geiping** (Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems): Safety and Security of LLM Reasoning, register on [Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/bliss-speaker-series/events/316827544) or [Luma](https://luma.com/mvyomo7c)
- **1 December: Jonathan Richard Schwarz** (Imperial College London): Sovereign AI from a Perspective of Model Building and AI Governance, register on [Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/bliss-speaker-series/events/316827563) or [Luma](https://luma.com/pday4uph)
- **15 December: Ida Momennejad** (Microsoft Research): Algorithmic Geometry of Flexible Cognition in Brains and AI, register on [Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/bliss-speaker-series/events/316827574) or [Luma](https://luma.com/u9sa8itp)
- **12 January: Robert McCarthy** (UCL): The Importance of Model Specs and Constitutions for AI Safety, register on [Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/bliss-speaker-series/events/316827601) or [Luma](https://luma.com/u07yyszx)
- **26 January: Nenad Tomašev** (Google DeepMind): Agentic Web: Opportunities and Challenges, register on [Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/bliss-speaker-series/events/316827615) or [Luma](https://luma.com/smyd2k1z)
- **9 February: Uljad Berdica** (University of Oxford, Google DeepMind): From Clean Slates to Many Worlds: Rethinking RL Algorithm Discovery, register on [Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/bliss-speaker-series/events/316827711) or [Luma](https://luma.com/q85gtef0)

Seats are limited and the doors close at 7:15 pm, so register early and come on time. All dates are also on the [Speaker Series page](/speaker-series). See you there!
